Questions, answered

Good to know before you begin

A few details about the HIREMIND AI product vision.

What is HIREMIND AI?

HIREMIND AI is a proposed recruitment workspace designed to help HR teams organise roles, candidate information, interview preparation and hiring workflows with responsible AI assistance.

Who is it designed for?

It is being designed for startups, growing businesses, enterprise HR teams, recruiters and talent acquisition professionals.

Does AI make hiring decisions?

No. The product direction is human-led. AI may help organise information and prepare materials, but authorised people must review evidence and make employment decisions.

Can we use our own hiring process?

The planned platform will be developed around configurable workflows. The first release will validate the most common needs before adding advanced customisation.

Is the platform available now?

This is the Phase 1 marketing website and product concept. Account registration, live AI tools, candidate databases and paid subscriptions are not connected yet.
